UNDERSTUDY

AI video now models gravity, contact and grasping well enough to look real at a glance. The natural next step is to stop treating it only as media and use it as data. Robotics first.

Robots learn skills by watching demonstrations, and they need a lot of them. Today people collect those by teleoperating robots or filming themselves, one take at a time: slow and expensive. Understudy makes the demonstrations from one sentence, so anyone can teach a low-cost arm (the LeRobot SO-101) a specific task. An understudy learns the role by watching the lead; here the lead is a person Runway made up.

Runway imagines the demonstration. Physics decides if it counts.

1PROMPT

>

One sentence in. The planner checks the SO-101 can do it at all (one 5-DOF arm, two fingers) before any credit is spent. These get refused:

  • fold the towel deformable, a 2-finger gripper cannot manage cloth
  • pour water into the cup liquids need wrist twist and are not rigid
  • use both hands to ... the SO-101 is one arm

2RUNWAY MAKES THE LEAD

gen4_image draws the first frame, gen4_image_turbo restyles it (table, bowl, light), gen4_turbo animates 5 s of a hand doing the task.

3SKELETONS

MediaPipe Hand Landmarker (21 points) and Pose heavy find the hand and arm on every frame; an OpenCV colour tracker finds the block. Grasp and release come from the block's own motion.

4STRICT GATES

Generated video is not always physical, which is why 21 gates stand between a clip and a training episode. The rejections are part of the result. Full spec in docs/DATA-SPEC.md.

5LEROBOT DATASET

The hand's grasp point becomes the SO-101's grasp point, 1:1 in metres (the 3 cm block is the ruler). Each accepted clip is replayed in MuJoCo with real contact physics, then written with LeRobot LeRobotDataset.create.

6THE UNDERSTUDY PERFORMS

A small policy (MLP, action chunks, ACT-style ensembling) trained on that dataset on a laptop CPU, then run in MuJoCo on cube positions it has not seen.

Built for the Runway API hackathon. Runway, MediaPipe, MuJoCo, LeRobot, Claude.
