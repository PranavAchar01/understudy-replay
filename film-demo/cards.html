Benchmark · honest caveats

What this does and does not show.

  • SIMAll results are in MuJoCo simulation.
  • BARThe baseline knows the exact cube pose: stronger than human teleoperation.
  • LOOPPrompts refined with a PhyT2V loop (Xue et al., CVPR 2025), our physics gates as the critic.
  • PRIORInspired by NVIDIA DreamGen (2025), which generates video of the robot itself. Understudy generates people, with any Runway model, and filters with physics.
